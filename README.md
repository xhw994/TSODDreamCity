# Dream City (梦境之城) — Civilization VI Mod

> A fan-made Civilization VI mod introducing **Dream City**, a city-state from the Chinese web novel *Sword of Dawn (黎明之剑)*, together with a brand-new city-state category: **Entertainment**.

## Overview

Dream City is a city in the novel's sandbox, watched over by the High Narrator, a vast spider whose threads run through every mind in the city. When the city was destroyed, the threads remained and became the Narrator Neural Network.

In the game it is an **Entertainment city-state**, the seventh category after Cultural, Industrial, Militaristic, Religious, Scientific and Trade. Its envoy bonuses reward Entertainment Complexes and Water Parks with amenities, and its suzerain bonus speeds up research and civics.

It is one of the mods in the *Sword of Dawn* series, alongside the Cecil Empire and Typhon Empire mods, and works on its own.

---

## City-State Features

### Category: Entertainment

| Envoys | Bonus |
|---|---|
| 1 | +1 Amenity in the **Capital** and in every **Arena** and **Ferris Wheel** |
| 3 | +1 Amenity in every **Zoo** and **Aquarium** |
| 6 | +1 Amenity in every **Stadium** and **Aquatics Center** |

Arena, Zoo and Stadium are Entertainment Complex buildings; Ferris Wheel, Aquarium and Aquatics Center are Water Park buildings. The amenities count as Amenities from city-states.

---

### Suzerain Bonus: *Narrator Neural Network*

- **Eurekas** and **Inspirations** provide an additional **10%** of technology and civic costs.
- Each **district** in your cities provides **+1 Amenity**.

---

## Recommended Play

| Victory Type | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| **Science** | Stronger Eurekas, and amenities that let your cities grow without stopping for happiness |
| **Culture** | Stronger Inspirations, and the same amenities for a wide empire |

Even one envoy pays off if you already build Entertainment Complexes or Water Parks. Becoming suzerain is worth it for the district amenities alone.

## Installation

### Steam Workshop *(recommended)*

1. Subscribe on the [Steam Workshop page](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3795364293).
2. Launch Civilization VI → **Additional Content** → enable **Dream City**.

### Manual Installation

1. Copy this folder (the one containing `DreamCity.modinfo`) to your Civ VI mods directory. No build step is needed; files the `.modinfo` does not list are ignored by the game.
   - **Windows:** `%USERPROFILE%\Documents\My Games\Sid Meier's Civilization VI\Mods\`
   - **macOS:** `~/Library/Application Support/Sid Meier's Civilization VI/Mods/`
2. Launch Civilization VI → **Additional Content** → enable **Dream City**.

### Requirements

- The **Gathering Storm** ruleset.
- Dream City is optional per game: in Game Setup, under Map Options, choose **Select City-States** and tick **Dream City**. Start a new game; city-states cannot be added to an existing save.

---

## Compatibility

- **Better City States** and **CIVITAS City-States:** both tested. With CIVITAS enabled, the Entertainment category is also registered in its Civilopedia.
- **Other city-state UI mods:** the mod replaces the City States panel script so the new category gets its icon and name, and chains with other mods that also change that panel following the community convention. A mod that replaces the panel outright may leave the Entertainment category with a blank icon and name.

If you find a conflict, please open an issue with your mod list and the `Lua.log` file.
